How Paw Cleaner works

Paw Cleaner lets you go through your camera roll one photo or video at a time: swipe left to delete, right to keep, up to favorite. Nothing is deleted until you confirm — the actual deletion happens through iOS's own confirmation dialog, not silently in the background.

Photos access & iCloud

Paw Cleaner works with the Photos permission you grant it — full library or a limited selection, your choice. You can change this anytime from the iOS Settings app. If your library uses "Optimize iPhone Storage," some photos may take a moment to load while iCloud downloads the full-resolution version.

Deleting photos

Swiped-away photos aren't gone immediately — like anywhere else on iOS, they move to the "Recently Deleted" album, where you can still recover them for a limited time before they're permanently removed.

Restoring Premium

If you've purchased Premium and it's not reflecting on a new device or after reinstalling, contact us at the address above and we'll help you get it restored.
