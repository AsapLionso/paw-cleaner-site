How it works

A few minutes a day, and it becomes a habit.

01
Pick what to clean
All your photos, the oldest ones, screenshots, duplicates, your videos…
02
Swipe
Right to keep, left to delete, up to favorite. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.
03
Your companion grows
Every day you sort, it gains 1 day. It evolves at 7 days, 15 days, 30 days…
04
Come back tomorrow
New quests, games and a chest are waiting for you every day.

Someone is waiting for you

The first day you reach 30 actions, a cat moves in with you. Give it a name. From then on, every day you sort your photos or videos, it gains a day and changes its look as it grows.

  2. Day 7
  3. Day 15

What shall we play?

Three mini-games to explore, one short break at a time. The more you sort, the happier your companion, and the more each game pays you.

Kibble

Catch the crocks. Dodge the surprises.

One 42-second round a day

Memory

Memorize the item and find it!

Six tiers, from Easy to Divine

Fishing

The right move at the right time.

Five baits a day, from day 7

A chest to open every day

Five quests a day: sort a few old photos, play together… Each one pays diamonds. Finish them all and the chest of the day is yours.

  • Finish the Photos Dice Roll +5
  • Use all 5 baits while fishing +11
  • Sort 5 old photos +8

Privacy

Your photos stay yours.

Paw Cleaner is designed to keep your photo library private. Your photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Your companion, its home and your progress live on your iPhone too.
